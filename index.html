Pitch it. Build it. Defend it.

A two-round innovation sprint that turns a raw problem statement into a working solution — judged, live, on the floor.

Register Now Rulebook
Overview

Ignite innovation and entrepreneurial zeal.

Ideathon is Aavishkar Manthan's flagship technical event — a platform for participants to showcase groundbreaking ideas and sharpen them into real solutions.

The event is designed to spark a whirlwind of creativity, offering a dynamic stage for teams to unveil concepts with genuine potential to move their field forward. It's judged by industry practitioners and academics who push participants to defend both the problem and the solution.

How it works

Two rounds, one arc.

  1. R1Problem Pitch — teams identify and pitch a real problem statement worth solving.
  2. R2Solution Reveal — shortlisted teams return to unveil their ingenious, workable solution.
Who it's for

Builders, thinkers, and first-time founders.

Open to student teams from any institution with an idea worth defending in front of a room — no prior startup experience required, just a problem you actually care about solving.

Where words do the winning.

A captivating non-technical event, giving participants a stage to eloquently express thought, perspective and personality.

Register Now Rulebook
Overview

Communication skill, tested from every angle.

Elocution is Aavishkar Manthan's non-technical flagship — a riveting platform for participants to think fast, speak sharper, and hold a room.

Round one runs through five distinct sub-rounds, each testing a different facet of communication and creativity, before narrowing to a single, high-stakes final: The Debate — where minds clash and ideas soar.

Format

Five sub-rounds. One final debate.

  1. 01Reverse Dictionary
  2. 02Twist Your Story
  3. 03Memory Play
  4. 04Insta-Riff Rally
  5. 05Inquiry
  6. FinalThe Debate — the ultimate showdown
Who it's for

Anyone with something worth saying.

Open to individual participants from any institution — no technical background needed, just sharp thinking and the nerve to speak up.

Every step, for the green planet.

A morning march in partnership with NSS MCE — turning footsteps into a message about sustainability.

Register Now
Overview

A walk with a purpose.

The Walkathon is Aavishkar Manthan's morning awareness event — organised in collaboration with the National Service Scheme (NSS) of MCE Hassan.

The route runs from MCE Hassan to the Hemavathi statue, followed by a street play performed by the MCE cultural team, highlighting the importance of saving the green planet. In its previous edition, nearly 2,000 participants joined the walk.

Purpose

Awareness, in motion.

Beyond the walk itself, the event is built to spark conversation — using a street performance to carry a sustainability message to everyone along the route, on and off campus.

Overview

The vibrant, grand finale.

Held in collaboration with the MCE cultural team, Bonfire serves as the closing chapter of Aavishkar Manthan — set against the backdrop of the Heragu Amphitheatre.

As the flames rise and the night deepens, the outdoor evening brings participants together for music, laughter, and unforgettable moments — a fitting close to two days of competition and community.

Expect live performances, cultural acts and an open, informal atmosphere — the one part of Aavishkar Manthan built purely for celebration.
